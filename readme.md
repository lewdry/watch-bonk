A simple, minimalist Garmin watch face with a colourful twist.

Bonk shows the time and nothing else, until you raise your wrist. Then colourful balls appear, different sizes and colors every time. Let your watch sleep, and they disappear.

*Business in front, balls in the back(ground).*
